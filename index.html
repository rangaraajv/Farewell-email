When I look back at this journey — which started with my first job at Accelalpha and eventually brought me to IBM — I honestly feel incredibly grateful. What started as a campus placement and my first salary slowly became one of the most important chapters of my life. I came here to build a career, but somewhere along the way, I built friendships, found mentors, found people I could call family, and collected memories that I know I will carry with me for a very long time.

There are far too many people who have been part of this journey, and if I start naming everyone, this page will probably never end. But there are a few people and groups whom I absolutely cannot leave without mentioning.

Nat Ganesh — the former CEO and Founder of Accelalpha, and the man because of whom I got my life's first job and my first salary. At that point, I don't think I understood how significant that opportunity would become in my life. Looking back today, I realise that one opportunity changed the entire direction of my career. I will always be grateful for that.

Jeeshan — a great leader who guided us and led us incredibly well, but more than that, a genuinely great human being. Someone I know I can trust, reach out to, and ask for help anytime. There are so many things I have learned from you, and honestly, I am still learning from you every day. Thank you for always being someone I could look up to.

Vinay — if there is one person whose name I cannot leave out when I talk about my career, it is you. You are the man because of whom I am something today. You shaped me completely. I openly say this — I genuinely don't know where I would have landed in my career if I had not met you. You were my mentor, my lead, my manager, and without any doubt, the best manager I have ever had. A very big part of whatever I am today professionally has your fingerprints on it. Thank you for believing in me, pushing me, correcting me, giving me opportunities, and most importantly, shaping me into the person I am today.

Sambit — what do I even write about him? He is my brother, and sometimes that one word is enough. He is one of those very few people whom I can call anytime, for anything, and no matter what, he has always stood by me. Some relationships don't need much explanation. This is one of them. Thank you for being there, always.

And then comes my AAU batch — Divakar, Siddanthan, Visnu Kumar, Tejas, Bhargav, Vikneswaran, Vignesh, Gokul, Sangmesh, Kallesha, Meghana, Nikhitha N A, Harika, Nikitha J, Roshni Kumari and Nivetha. You guys were never just colleagues. You were family. These were the friendships that happened in my very first workplace, and somehow we never really felt like we had come to work. It always felt like we had come to college. The lunch breaks, random conversations, celebrations, teasing each other, helping each other and all the nonsense we did together — those are some of the memories I will miss the most. I am genuinely lucky that my first workplace gave me a group of people like you.

Then comes my first domain and the beginning of my actual career — CPQ.

Latha Muthu — the woman who brought Accelalpha to our college for recruitment and, indirectly, became one of the biggest reasons I am here today. Thank you for giving us that opportunity.

And to Sarath, Malli, Aniruth, Asif, Sambit, Priyanka Desai, Priya Nair and Kavitha Kothari, and everyone who was part of that CPQ journey — thank you for giving me my first exposure to the professional world and for everything I learned from you.

My first teammates — Karthik S, Manasi, Preeti, Alex, Vijayalaxmi and everyone else who was part of that journey — thank you for making those early days so memorable.

And Yashwanth Kumar Guntur — my first trainer, my first lead, and the first person under whom I actually started working in my career. You were literally there when I was learning how to begin this journey. I will always remember that.

Then came my technology journey, and of course, Vinay appears here again. He was not just the person who managed me; he was one of the people who helped build the technical professional I became.

To my first tech teammates from the Pilot Batch of Capital Projects — Priyanka Gadekar, Dnaynsudha, Sid, Divakar, Vikki and Visnu — thank you for being part of those early technical days and for everything we learned together.

Amit — our silent leader, but always very supportive. Thank you for being someone we could always count on.

Rabindra — one of the finest team leads I have worked under, and someone who gave me the opportunity to learn and grow during Martin Brothers.

And my Martin Brothers team — Swathi, Manikanta, Soundarya and Venkatesh. Swathi — the woman behind a very important part of my OIC debugging skills 😄. Manikanta — the man who stood with me whenever I needed him, both in the project and in life. Soundarya and Venkatesh — thank you for being part of that chapter.

And Shailesh Potnis, my onshore lead — you were a very strong pillar in my career and played a huge role in helping me get into the project, understand the work, and perform well. Thank you for the trust and support.

Then came Wieland. Raghav — thank you for giving me the freedom to design the integrations the way I wanted and, more importantly, for giving me the confidence that I could actually architect integrations myself. Your support, sharpness and trust made a huge difference to me.

Shashank and Sarath — thank you for giving me the opportunity to once again look into and work with the CPQ environment, something that brought me back to where my career started. And Hitendra from EBS — thank you for being part of this chapter as well.

I also want to take a moment to thank the PMO team, who have always been such an energetic part of our organisation. Arul — still the backbone of the GE project. Achyut — the person I worked with most and someone who was always much more than just a PM. Divya — the most recent and the last PM I worked under. And Deepa and Purushotam — although I didn't get the opportunity to work with you directly, thank you for being part of the journey.

And then my all-time favourite HR team. Daragesh — more like an elder brother to us than just HR. Sam — the guy who could always entertain us by doing something random 😄. Gayatri — the only HR who somehow still looks like a school student 😂. Karthik Rallabandi — the wanderer, because of whom we don't have to take the stress of booking stays or planning travel 😄. And Ranvir Paaji — how can I ever forget you, Paaji? Thank you for everything. ❤️

A special thanks to our IT guys, Sameer and Kiran, who were always helpful and supportive whenever we needed them.

And finally, my juniors and AAU Batch 2 — Guru, Shibi, Manoj, Nanda, Aravind, Manivasan, Rajkumar, Srinath, Sakthi, Saran, Pushpamala, Nuthan, Nachiketh and Gopi Krishna. Thank you for all the memories, conversations, fun and madness. It was always great having you guys around.

There are also so many other practice leaders, colleagues, friends and people I have worked with, interacted with, learned from, or simply shared a few good moments with. Not mentioning your name does not mean you were any less important to my journey.

When I joined Accelalpha, I was just a fresher looking for his first opportunity. Today, as I move on, I carry with me experiences from different projects, different technologies, different teams and, most importantly, relationships with some incredible people.

I came here to build a career. I never expected to build a family along the way.

That's probably what makes leaving difficult.

I will miss the office, the random conversations, lunch breaks, celebrations, project discussions, last-minute calls, debugging sessions, the jokes, the arguments, the chai/coffee breaks and all those small everyday moments that we never realise are special until they become memories.

I am excited about what comes next, but I will always be grateful for what came before. This may be the end of one chapter, but it definitely isn't the end of the relationships we built here. You all know I am just a phone call away. No matter where we are or how busy life gets, if you ever need me, call me.

This is not goodbye.
It is simply… until next time. ❤️

With love,

The Journey

Chapters of
a Career Built

Chapter 01 · The Beginning
Campus → Accelalpha
It started as a campus placement — a fresher looking for his first opportunity. Nat Ganesh gave me that chance, and from the very first day, it felt less like a workplace and more like home. The AAU Batch made sure of that.
Chapter 02 · First Domain
CPQ — Where It All Began
Latha Muthu brought Accelalpha to our college. Yashwanth was my first lead. Sarath, Malli, Aniruth, Asif and the rest of the CPQ team gave me my first real exposure to the professional world.
Chapter 03 · The Technical Leap
Capital Projects Pilot Batch
Vinay entered. Everything changed. The Pilot Batch — Priyanka Gadekar, Dnaynsudha, Sid, Divakar, Vikki and Visnu — were my first tech teammates. Amit was our quiet, steady anchor.
Chapter 04 · Growth
Martin Brothers
Rabindra led us brilliantly. Shailesh Potnis was the onshore pillar. Swathi taught me OIC debugging more than any documentation ever could. Manikanta was always there when it mattered.
Chapter 05 · Integration Guy
Wieland — Designing My Own Way
Raghav gave me freedom. I designed integrations the way I believed they should be built. Shashank and Sarath brought CPQ back into the picture. Hitendra from EBS completed the team.
Chapter 06 · IBM
And Beyond
From a campus recruit to an Integration Developer at IBM. Every project, every team, every debugging session, every chai break — all of it brought me here. Grateful doesn't even begin to cover it.
